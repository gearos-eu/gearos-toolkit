Common questions

Do I need a Claude subscription?

Yes. GearOS is a plugin for Claude Code, which requires an Anthropic account. A Pro plan ($20/mo) is sufficient for most dealers.

Does GearOS send emails automatically?

No. Every email, draft, or message requires your explicit approval before anything is sent. GearOS generates, you decide.

Does it work with AutoScout24?

Yes. The car-listing-scanner and competitor-pricing-monitor include AutoScout24 as a fallback when mobile.de is unavailable.

Where is my data stored?

Everything runs locally on your machine. GearOS does not store your vehicle data, contact lists, or emails. The only external call is the license key validation.

What countries does it support?

GearOS is built for dealers sourcing from Germany (DE) and selling in SK, CZ, PL, HU, RO, and other EU markets. Language support: DE, EN, SK, CS, PL, HU, RO, FR, IT.

Can I customize it?

Yes. All behavior is controlled by config files — no coding required. For custom skills or white-label builds, contact us about the Agency tier.
